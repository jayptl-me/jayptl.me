## The short version

I'm a founding-engineer-grade full-stack developer from Anand, Gujarat, with 4+ years across TypeScript, Dart/Flutter, Python, and Solidity. I've built production apps spanning Flutter, React Native, Next.js, Angular, and Astro — backed by Bun + Hono APIs running on infrastructure I migrated off big cloud bills onto Docker, Coolify, and Traefik myself.

On the research side, I train small language models from scratch ([Vini and Tini](/projects/vini-tini.md), BitNet b1.58 ternary architecture) for edge deployment — one of them is published on HuggingFace.

Along the way: first place in the FinTech track at CHARUSAT-Odoo Hackathon 2025 out of 650+ applicants, a Technical Leadership Award, certifications across Google Cloud, AWS, MongoDB, Meta, and DataCamp, and a B.Tech in Information Technology from A.D. Patel Institute of Technology.

If it ships, I can build it, design it, plan it, film it, and make the launch trailer for it.
