05

Hills I will die on

  1. Ship the boring version first. The clever version can wait until real people are using the boring one.
  2. Fake numbers are worse than no numbers. If a dashboard, a demo or a resume cannot back a figure, the figure goes.
  3. Frameworks are a choice, not a starting point. This site has none on purpose; most products need one, and should say why.
  4. Own your infrastructure early. A cloud bill is a design review you pay for every month.
  5. Motion should explain, not decorate. If an animation does not help you read, it does not ship.
